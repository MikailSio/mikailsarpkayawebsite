ABOUT
ME

I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from Osmaniye Korkut Ata University and I'm currently pursuing my M.Sc. at the same department. My thesis focuses on sentiment analysis of English IMDb reviews using deep learning and transformer architectures.

For my research I use PyTorch and HuggingFace Transformers for model training, Scikit-learn for classical ML, and Pandas / NumPy for data processing. I also have experience with C, C++, and C#.

Beyond research, I build and deploy web applications on a Linux VPS with Nginx & Cloudflare — including abctohero.com, a cross-platform English learning app supporting 9 native languages. I also maintain a series of 44 interactive AI/ML tutorials (442 lessons) on this site.
