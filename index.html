02 — Profile

ABOUT
ME

I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from Osmaniye Korkut Ata University (2020) and I'm currently pursuing my M.Sc. at the same department. My thesis investigates transformer-based architectures — including BERT, RoBERTa, and hybrid CNN-LSTM models — for Turkish text classification tasks.

My research pipeline spans the full cycle: automated web scraping with BeautifulSoup & Scrapy, large-scale data cleaning with Pandas, corpus construction for low-resource languages, and model training via PyTorch & HuggingFace Transformers. I co-author journal papers with faculty advisors on NLP-driven classification systems.

Beyond research, I build and deploy production web applications on Hetzner VPS with Nginx & Cloudflare — including abctohero.com, an interactive English learning platform. I hold professional certificates from Stanford, IBM, Google, and DeepLearning.AI across ML, DL, and data analytics.
