About me

My academic journey started at Osmaniye Korkut Ata University with Electrical & Electronics Engineering; now I'm continuing with my M.Sc. in the same department, focused on NLP. Next year I'm spending two semesters at Tarragona / URV on Erasmus — I'll be working on my M.Sc. thesis there while taking courses from their Computer Security Engineering and AI program. That's why I've been doubling down on math foundations and cybersecurity lately.

The tutorials and projects here — from Python basics to transformer architectures — are records I keep to lock down what I'm learning. I come back to them often — adding what I've newly learned and emerging technologies as they come, so my knowledge stays fresh. I learn by explaining; AI helps along the way.

I don't enjoy keeping things just in theory — building and sharing is the real fun. I run a few projects on a Linux VPS with Nginx & Cloudflare; the one I care about most is abctohero.com, a platform I built to learn English while watching TV shows. From 60 series, I extracted 130k+ raw words from the subtitles and filtered them down to a 54k+ word pool — the important root words have their own etymology pages.

On the academic side, I'm a research scholarship holder in a TÜBİTAK 1001 project led by my advisor (area: machine learning for rehabilitation devices).
